Research

My thesis investigates transition-sensitive hybrid RANS–LES modelling across canonical bluff-body and low-Reynolds-number aerodynamic configurations.

01 — MODEL DEVELOPMENT

BCM-DES

Coupling the SA-BCM transition model with the SA-DDES framework through the blending parameter Φ = fd × γBC.

02 — SOLVER

PRAVAHA-IITH

Simulations performed using an in-house compressible unstructured finite-volume CFD solver.

03 — BLUFF BODY

Drag Crisis

Stationary circular cylinder simulations from Re = 5×10⁴ to 10⁶ to investigate transition-induced drag reduction.

04 — ROTATING FLOW

Lift Crisis

Rotating-cylinder simulations at Re = 152,000 across multiple spin ratios to investigate rotation-induced asymmetric transition.

05 — AIRFOIL

Eppler 387

Low-Reynolds-number airfoil simulations examining laminar separation bubbles, transition and static stall.

06 — FLOW PHYSICS

Q-Criterion

Three-dimensional wake structures and turbulent flow features investigated through CFD visualization.
